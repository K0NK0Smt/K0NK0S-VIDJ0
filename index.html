Mic uses your microphone — pick a specific input from the dropdown above, or leave it on Default. Runs fine at the same time as either camera source, including the back camera. Device Audio captures system/tab sound where the browser supports it (desktop Chrome/Edge) — pick a tab and enable "Share audio" in the picker; it also runs alongside the camera, front or back, since it's a completely separate stream. Device Audio isn't available on iOS Safari or most mobile browsers (no screen-capture API) — use Mic there instead. Each layer below also has its own AR (Audio React) toggle to pulse its opacity and scale with the signal.

Three independent oscillator lanes, each with its own wave/rate/depth. Pick a lane above, then toggle which parameters it drives — a parameter can be driven by more than one lane at once, and their motion adds together. The bracketed numbers on a lit-up target show which lane(s) currently control it. Stack lanes with Audio reactivity on shared targets for combined motion. Layers get their own separate LFO too.
